# Gradual Abrasion and Spore Debris Particles

Single-pass full-alpha erasure caused mold to instantly vanish like a cookie cutter, eliminating the tactile sensation of physical scrubbing. We decided to implement gradual multi-pass abrasion (reducing destination alpha by ~0.15–0.32 per pass depending on cursor velocity, requiring 3–4 strokes in the same location for full sanitization) combined with an ephemeral spore debris particle canvas overlay that scatters flecks of mold dust during vigorous friction.
